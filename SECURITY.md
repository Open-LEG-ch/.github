# Security Policy

OpenLEG handles citizen smart-meter data. We take that seriously, so security reports get priority.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a security problem. Email **hallo@openleg.ch** with the details:

- what you found and where,
- how to reproduce it,
- the impact you expect.

We confirm receipt within a few working days and keep you posted while we fix it. Once a patch ships, we are glad to credit you, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope

Anything in the OpenLEG codebase and the services it runs: the Flask app, the API, the database layer, and the deployment templates in this organisation.

## Our data line

Citizen smart-meter data stays inside each local energy community. We never sell it and never aggregate it for third parties. A report that exposes such data is the most serious kind we handle.
