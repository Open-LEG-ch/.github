# Support

Need help with OpenLEG? Here is where to go.

- **A bug or a concrete problem**: open an issue in the relevant repository.
- **A question or an idea**: start a discussion, or open an issue and label it `question`.
- **Planning a real LEG and stuck**: email **hallo@openleg.ch** and tell us your municipality and what you are trying to do.
- **Security problem**: do not post it publicly, see `SECURITY.md`.

We are a small open-source project, so answers take time. Clear steps and context get you a faster reply.
